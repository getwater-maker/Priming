# 구글 브랜딩 인증 문의 초안 (2026-10-19)

> 상황: 인증 센터의 「Prepare for verification」이 회색이고 브랜딩 화면에 「Verify Branding」 버튼이 없다. 구글 개발자 포럼에 **똑같은 증상** 글이 여러 건 있다(아래 링크) — 로이 쪽 설정 문제가 아니라 구글 쪽 현상일 가능성이 크다. 공식 문서는 「브랜딩이 인증이 필요하면 Verify Branding 버튼이 보인다」고만 한다.
> 쓰는 곳: 구글 클라우드 커뮤니티 `discuss.google.dev` 에 새 글(구글 직원·Trust & Safety 가 보는 곳). adonairoy2026 계정으로 로그인해서 올린다. **글을 올리는 것은 로이가 한다.**

## 올릴 글 (영어)

**Title:** Verification Center: "Prepare for verification" disabled and no "Verify Branding" button (External, In production, branding complete) — project priming-upload

**Body:**

Our OAuth app cannot start verification. The Verification Center says "You need to verify and publish your branding before you can request verification", but the Branding page has no "Verify Branding" or "Publish branding" button — only Save and Discard.

- Project ID: `priming-upload` (project number 932586314031)
- User type: External · Publishing status: In production
- Sensitive scopes: `youtube.upload`, `youtube.readonly`, `youtube.force-ssl`
- Branding (all saved, reloaded to confirm): app name "Priming", app logo (640x640 PNG), homepage `https://www.primingwave.com/priming`, privacy policy `https://www.primingwave.com/privacy`, terms `https://www.primingwave.com/terms`, authorized domain `primingwave.com` (ownership verified in Search Console with the project owner account), developer contact email set.
- The homepage is public (no login), describes the app and its YouTube features, and links to the privacy policy at the same URL configured in Branding. The privacy policy is on the same domain and includes a YouTube API Services section (YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy links, scopes, storage, revocation, Limited Use statement).
- Data access page: scope justification and a demo video link are saved.
- Verification Center status: "Branding isn't shown to users" and "Prepare for verification" stays greyed out. It has been like this for more than 24 hours after the last branding change.

We saw the same symptom in other threads (links below), so this looks like the branding review is not being triggered rather than a content problem. Could someone from the Google Auth Platform / Trust & Safety team check the branding state of project `priming-upload` and trigger the branding review (or tell us what is missing)? Thank you.
